01 / HOW I WORK

Three ideas that keep showing up in my engineering.

01 / CONSTRAINTS

Start with the environment.

Connectivity, hardware and infrastructure are design inputs, not edge cases. The system should reflect the conditions its users actually face.

02 / TRADEOFFS

Make decisions explicit.

Architecture becomes more useful when the cost, failure mode and reason behind a decision are visible instead of hidden behind technology names.

03 / DELIVERY

Stay close to implementation.

Technical leadership is stronger when strategy remains connected to shipping, testing and the day-to-day reality of the engineering team.
